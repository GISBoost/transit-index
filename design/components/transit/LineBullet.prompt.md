Colored route-bullet disc with a letter/number — brand identifier for editions, sections, cities.
```jsx
<LineBullet label="01" line="vermilion" />
<LineBullet label="PL" line="blue" size="lg" />
<LineBullet label="7" line="violet" shape="diamond" />
```
Keys: vermilion, orange, yellow, green, sky, blue, violet, black. Colors are decoration — for data use the speed scale (`--speed-1…5`). Text auto-switches to ink on yellow/orange/sky.
