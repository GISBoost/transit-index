Transit Index mark ("t" — a 2-line bundle with one concentric bend, crossed once by a bar with a knocked-out gap) and horizontal lockup.
```jsx
<Logo size={40} />
<Logo variant="mark" size={16} tone="mono" />
<div data-theme="night"><Logo credit /></div>
```

Color follows theme tokens; mono uses currentColor. Gaps are masks, so the mark works on any background. Static files in `assets/logo/`.
